## 📌 Overview
**ShramikSetu AI** is a state-of-the-art GovTech platform engineered to empower India's 450+ million informal and migrant workforce. By combining deterministic rule engines, **Google Gemini AI**, and **In-Browser Web Speech Capabilities**, ShramikSetu AI bridges the gap between low-literacy workers, labor contractors, and government administrators.

---

## 🤖 12-Agent AI Ecosystem Overview

1. **Agent 1 (Voice Onboarding):** Speech-to-text in Hindi & Gujarati for form-free skill profile setup.
2. **Agent 2 (Welfare Scheme Engine):** Deterministic matching for Gujarat Govt schemes (BOCW, Manav Kalyan Yojana).
3. **Agent 3 (Wage Fairness Checker):** Audits daily pay against legal minimum wage thresholds.
4. **Agent 4 (e-Nyay Risk Scoring):** Classifies grievances into severity tiers and risk points.
5. **Agent 5 (Admin Command Center):** Multi-graph risk analytics, wage surveillance & direct worker messaging.
6. **Agent 6 (Contractor Job Broadcast):** Auto-fans out job alerts to matching eligible workers in district.
7. **Agent 7 (e-Nyay AI Legal Notice):** Generates official legal dispute slips citing Indian Labor Laws.
8. **Agent 8 (Kaushalya Path Wage Doubler):** Maps skills to Govt ITI & Kaushalya University courses to double daily pay.
9. **Agent 9 (Contractor TrustScore):** 0-100 Fair Employer Rating Engine on job broadcasts.
10. **Agent 10 (Suraksha Guard SOS):** Detects critical danger keywords and triggers instant Admin Red SOS banners.
11. **Agent 13 (Bhasha Mitra Voice Translator):** Real-time bi-directional voice translation with Audio Text-to-Speech.
12. **Agent 14 (HakKosh Digital Wage Contract):** Generates tamper-proof Digital Employment Agreements locking daily wages.
